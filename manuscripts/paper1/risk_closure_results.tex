The development rule selects $(g_t,g_a)=(1.0,0.5)$ for the hybrid and $(1.3,0.5)$ for matched PID. Table~\ref{tab:risk-validation} reports the independent data, while Figure~\ref{fig:risk-frontier} shows every development candidate. Selection uses the declared scalar preference; the nondominated points expose alternative tracking, holding, discharge and switching compromises.

At 0.5 ms the selected hybrid passes 10/10 and matched PID passes 10/10. Their mean hold drops are 2.109/3.556 mm, tracking RMSE 1.273/0.314 mm, discharge 23.79/35.29 mL, and changes 54.2/7.0. Paired differences and uncertainty intervals are given in S10. These data quantify the chosen policies rather than establishing algorithm-wide superiority.

Disabling storage in the selected hybrid at 1 ms gives 10/10 passing paths, mean drop 2.206 mm and worst error 8.469 mm. Together with the frozen-gain ablation, this does not establish that storage is necessary. The evaluated contribution is therefore the observation/supervision protocol and its explicit trade-offs; no indispensable accumulator or energy-saving claim is made.
