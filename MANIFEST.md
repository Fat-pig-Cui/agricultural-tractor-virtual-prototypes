# Public archive scope

This snapshot records the two published articles and their clean source-level reproduction materials.

- Paper 1 is published in *Machines* 2026, 14(11), 1248, DOI `10.3390/machines14111248`.
- Paper 2 is published in *Energies* 2026, 19(21), 5024, DOI `10.3390/en19215024`.
- The checked-in manuscript PDFs are the published PDFs supplied by the authors.
- TeX source files contain no editor queries, reviewer responses or proof-highlighting wrappers.
- Source code, declared parameters, figures and documentation are included.
- Frozen experiment outputs, trajectories, checkpoints, review correspondence, certificates and submission packages are excluded.
- The two articles remain theoretical virtual-prototype studies; the archive does not turn them into hardware validation.

`MANIFEST.sha256` covers all tracked public files except itself. `tools/verify_release.py` checks file hashes, source syntax and the exclusion policy.
